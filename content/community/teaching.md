# Free Teaching Material

This page collects **freely available** Modelica courseware and learning packs—lecture slides, exercises, open courseware, interactive tutorials, and video series that you can open without a paid enrollment.

For classic language guides and the free online book *Modelica by Example*, see [Learning the Modelica Language](/language/).  
For monographs and textbooks, see [Books](/publications/books/).  
For commercial instructor-led training, see [Training Courses](/community/training/).

If you offer free teaching material and would like it listed, please use the [contact form](/imprint/).
