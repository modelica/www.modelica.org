# American Modelica & FMI Conference 2026: highlights and photos

[Return to the newsletter report](../#american-modelica--fmi-conference-2026-a-report-from-atlanta)

The **American Modelica & FMI Conference 2026** took place on October 12–14 at the Georgia Institute of Technology’s Aerospace Systems Design Laboratory in Atlanta. Organized by the North American Modelica Users Group (NAMUG) in cooperation with the Modelica Association, the program brought together workshops, keynotes, paper and industrial presentations, vendor presentations, and an exhibition.

## Workshops

The conference opened with six workshops covering OpenModelica, the Modelica Buildings Library, Rumoca, Julia/Dyad, Modelon Impact, and SMArtInt+. Topics included modeling and simulation, equation-oriented compiler tools, agentic modeling workflows, and the generation and integration of neural networks and surrogate models.

## Keynotes and technical program

**Oliver Lenord**, Bosch Corporate Research, presented **“Pushing the Limits by Open Standards: Enabling Larger, Faster, and More Credible System Simulations.”** His talk addressed scalability, compiler improvements, scientific machine learning, and credible simulation workflows using open standards.

**Dr. Daniel Mikkelson**, Idaho National Laboratory, presented **“Nuclear System Modeling for Integrated Energy Systems Analyses,”** discussing dynamic modeling of nuclear energy systems and their integration with thermal and electrical applications.

The technical program included **27 paper presentations and eight industrial presentations**. Sessions covered FMI, building systems, emerging tools, power systems, automotive applications, data centers, and novel applications. A new track on equation-oriented languages beyond Modelica broadened the scope of the program.

## Paper awards

The **Best Paper Award** went to **“Towards an FMI Layered Standard for DAE – Applications for Simulation and Optimization”** by Elmir Nahodovic, Andreas Heuermann, Joel Andersson, Adwait Verulkar, Srikanth Sivaramakrishnan, Masoud Najafi, Linus Langenkamp, Christian Bertsch, Torsten Sommer, Erik Henningsson, Hans Olsson, and Bernhard Bachmann.

![Joel Andersson presenting the Best Paper Award winning paper](../american2026-best-paper-joel-andersson.jpg)

The **Best Student Paper Award** went to **“MMChvdcLibrary: Modeling Arm Average Value Modular Multilevel Converters in Modelica for Power System Simulation”** by **Md Nazmul Hassan Chowdhury and Luigi Vanfretti**.
